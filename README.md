
# RetailPulse Analytics 2025

## Retail Sales Data Analysis & Business Intelligence Project

RetailPulse Analytics 2025 is an end-to-end retail sales analytics and business intelligence project focused on analyzing sales, profit, customer behavior, product performance, regional trends, and business scenarios.

The project combines **Python, Oracle SQL, Excel, and Power BI** to transform retail sales data into meaningful business insights and decision-support outputs.

---

## Project Objectives

* Analyze retail sales and profit performance.
* Identify trends across products, categories, regions, and payment modes.
* Calculate and evaluate key business KPIs.
* Perform data analysis and visualization using multiple analytical tools.
* Use What-If Analysis to evaluate alternative business scenarios.
* Perform Sensitivity Analysis to understand the impact of discount changes on profit.
* Develop a Power BI dashboard for interactive business reporting.
* Present findings and recommendations in a structured business report.

## Analysis Performed

### Python Analysis

Python was used for:

* Dataset generation and preparation
* Data exploration
* Sales and profit analysis
* Category-level analysis
* Regional analysis
* Payment-mode analysis
* Monthly performance analysis
* Data visualization

The Python notebooks are available in the `02_Python` folder.

### SQL Analysis

Oracle SQL was used to perform structured business queries and analyze the retail sales dataset.

The SQL queries are available in:

`04_SQL/Retail_Sales.sql`

### Excel Analysis

Excel was used for:

* PivotTable analysis
* KPI calculations
* Sales and profit analysis
* What-If Analysis
* Sensitivity Analysis

The final Excel workbook is available in the `03_Excel` folder.

### What-If Analysis

What-If Analysis was performed to evaluate different business scenarios and understand how changes in assumptions can affect the final business outcome.

### Sensitivity Analysis

Sensitivity Analysis was performed by varying the **discount percentage** and observing its impact on **total profit**.

The analysis demonstrates that increasing discounts can significantly reduce profitability, making discount management an important business consideration.

### Power BI Dashboard

Power BI was used to create an interactive dashboard for monitoring retail sales performance and key business metrics.

The dashboard provides a consolidated view of the analyzed retail data.

## Business Insights

The project demonstrates how retail sales data can be used to identify performance patterns and support business decision-making.

The analysis particularly highlights the importance of:

* Monitoring sales and profitability together.
* Understanding product and category performance.
* Comparing regional and payment-mode performance.
* Tracking performance over time.
* Evaluating the financial impact of business assumptions.
* Using scenario and sensitivity analysis before making pricing or discount decisions.

---

## Business Recommendations

Based on the analytical framework developed in this project:

1. **Monitor profitability along with sales** rather than focusing only on revenue growth.
2. **Evaluate discount strategies carefully**, as higher discounts can reduce profit.
3. **Identify high-performing categories and products** and use their performance to guide business decisions.
4. **Monitor regional performance** to identify strong markets and areas requiring improvement.
5. **Use dashboards and KPIs regularly** for faster performance monitoring.
6. **Apply What-If and Sensitivity Analysis** before implementing major pricing or discount changes.
